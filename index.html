Frequently Asked Questions

Is the online reservation token amount 100% refundable?

Yes! When you reserve a plot online with a ₹25,000 token, your plot and price are locked for 15 days. If you choose not to proceed after visiting the site or reviewing the physical legal papers, 100% of the token is refunded to your bank account within 3 working days.

Can I get a bank loan for purchasing an open plot?

Absolutely. All our CRDA, HMDA, and DTCP approved residential ventures are pre-approved by leading banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis) for up to 75% plot purchase loans.

Are there any brokerage charges for buyers?

Zero brokerage. You buy directly at developer-authorized transparent pricing with complete clarity on per-square-yard rates and government registration charges.
